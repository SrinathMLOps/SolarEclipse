📍 Eclipse Path:

The dark shadow (umbra) moves from west to east across the Atlantic Ocean, passing over Iceland and parts of Spain. The animation shows how Earth's rotation and the Moon's orbit combine to create the eclipse shadow path. Locations within the shadow experience totality, while surrounding areas see a partial eclipse.
