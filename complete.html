🌑 What Is A Solar Eclipse?

The Alignment

☀️ → 🌑 → 🌍

A solar eclipse happens when the Moon moves between the Sun and Earth, blocking the Sun's light and casting a shadow on Earth.

Perfect Timing

The Moon is 400 times smaller than the Sun
But also 400 times closer to Earth
Result: They appear the same size in the sky!

This cosmic coincidence makes total solar eclipses possible.

The Shadows

🌑 Umbra: The darkest shadow where you see total eclipse

🌘 Penumbra: The lighter outer shadow where you see partial eclipse

The shadow races across Earth at 2,000 km/h!
